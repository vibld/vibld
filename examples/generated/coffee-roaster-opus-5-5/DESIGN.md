# Design

A marketing page for Wrenfield Coffee Roasters, a small-batch roaster in Asheville, North Carolina, aimed at home brewers who have never bought coffee by subscription, with one job: getting them to start a subscription.

The frontmatter above is this project's design spec: every colour, font, type step, space, radius and effect it uses, each section's copy verbatim, and what changes at each breakpoint. Change the spec and the code together.

## Do

- Keep terracotta #B5533A for meaning: the primary button, eyebrows on the #FAF8F3 ground, the 48 numeral, the bean temperature line and the pull-quote rule.
- Put cards on #FFFFFF over #FAF8F3 or #EFEAE1 so they lift by being lighter, with shadow-medium only on the roast card, the form card and the select popover.
- Enter content with opacity plus a 16-20px rise over 0.35s on cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1), and drive interaction with the stiffness 300, damping 35 spring.
- Set headings in Fraunces through the --text-* tokens (display -0.03em, headline -0.025em) and body copy in Source Sans 3 at 1.0625rem/1.6.
- Hold every section to max-w-page (78rem) with 20px/40px gutters, and let only the roast card and the coffee rail break out of it.

## Don't

- A second primary action such as a gift shop or wholesale button: starting a subscription is the page's one job, and its button appears only in the hero and the subscribe form.
- Showing the quotes without the placeholder notice: it would present invented customers as real ones.
- Terracotta text on #E8E0D5 or #EFEAE1: it falls to about 3.8:1 and fails the 4.5:1 contrast floor, so eyebrows there use #5C5850.
- Bouncy springs, scale-from-zero entrances or anything faster than 150ms on repeat actions: the paper register is unhurried and nothing bounces.
- Pure #FFFFFF as the page ground: the cards would stop lifting off the page.

## Checks

- At 1440px the headline sits on exactly two lines, 'Roasted Tuesday.' and 'Here by Saturday.'
- The 'Start a subscription' button appears twice on the page: once in the hero and once in the subscribe form.
- The testimonials band shows 'Placeholder quotes: these stand in for real subscriber reviews and will be replaced before launch.' with an info icon.
- Submitting the empty form shows the email error with an alert icon directly under the email field and moves focus to that field.
- A valid submission disables the button, shows a spinner with 'Checking your details' for about 1.2s, then 'Demo complete: nothing was sent.'
- At 375px the page never scrolls horizontally; only the coffee rail scrolls sideways, and it snaps card by card.
- With a mouse, the roast card tilts a few degrees and a terracotta light follows the pointer; with reduced motion on, the card neither tilts nor parallaxes and the marquee stands still.
