vibld 0.5.0: the open-source AI app builder that turns a description into a conventional React and TypeScript project you can read, run and take with you. This release adds checkpoint history, says what a build will cost before you send it, organizes the templates into categories, and brings in the plans and billing app.vibld.com launches with.

This is the code running at [app.vibld.com](https://app.vibld.com), a public beta. See [Status](https://github.com/vibld/vibld#status) for what works and what does not yet.

## Building

- **Checkpoint history.** The newest 100 checkpoints a project has accepted are listed, newest first, and any one of them can be restored in a single step. A restore never deletes a checkpoint, so restoring is itself undoable. It is refused while a build runs.
- **The cost of a build, before you send it.** The composer shows, beside Send, what a build on the chosen model is expected to cost, from the last 30 days of builds on that model. A model with too few builds shows the most a build can cost, as "Up to".
- **Claude Sonnet 5.5** is offered and is now the Sonnet family's default. Sonnet 5 stays available.
- **Fewer failed builds on Radix components.** The prompt now spells out how radix-ui's exports are imported and how `asChild` renders.

## Templates and styles

- **Templates by category.** Websites, Apps and App screens, each split into subcategories (Ecommerce, Portfolio, Internal tools and more), with a page per subcategory at [vibld.com/templates](https://vibld.com/templates). The builder's template picker uses the same categories.
- **The 1,300+ gallery styles are templates too.** Each style is a card under the website subcategory its industry names, on vibld.com and in the builder.
- **A sample gallery** on [vibld.com/examples](https://vibld.com/examples): five site types in up to six styles each, every one a real build you can open.

## Plans and billing (deployments that sell plans)

These apply only where a deployment has Stripe set up, as app.vibld.com does. A self-hosted copy without Stripe has no plans and no limits from them.

- **Free:** a one-time $0.20 trial, then $1 of model spend a month once a card is saved. Free builds share their own part of the deployment's daily budget (`VIBLD_FREE_DAILY_MICRO_USD`, $10 by default), so free use cannot pause paying accounts, and Free previews run for two hours a day (`VIBLD_FREE_PREVIEW_DAILY_MINUTES`).
- **Build** is $19 a month with $14 of model spend, **Ship** $49 a month with $40, and a **top-up** is $10 for $8 of spend.
- **Auto-reload**, off unless you turn it on: the $10 top-up is bought on your saved card when your balance runs low, up to a monthly cap you choose.
- **Start Build when Free runs out**, off unless you turn it on: moves a Free account to the Build plan on its saved card, once, when what it can spend drops below $1.
- The one-time welcome credit for saving a card is retired; the card now unlocks Free's monthly dollar instead.

## Fixes

- vibld.com: no sideways scrolling on mobile across the template pages and two docs pages, and each template's use-case link goes to its use-case page.
- vibld.com: the unused waitlist endpoint is removed.
- The self-hosting docs say what an unservable `VIBLD_MODEL` does and list every setting Docker reads.
- Dependency patches: source-map-js 1.2.2 in both starter templates (CVE-2026-93749) and sharp 0.35.5.

## Known limits

- **Self-hosting is checked by the project, not yet by anyone outside it.** Nobody has deployed to Render or run `cloud-init.yaml` on a real server yet.
- **Not under Docker:** the nightly cleanup jobs, Stripe, GitHub push and email.
- **The builder's Console and Problems panes** do not show the sandbox's output yet, and say so.
- **Repository search over an existing codebase is not built.**
